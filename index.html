HOW TO PLAY

COLOR FLOW Rules

Goal

Be the first player in a round to have 5 different colors on your own ends of the shared columns. Once you do that, the round ends and you get points for having more colors and less cards then your opponent. The first player to reach 25 points wins the match.

Score = 5 × (5 − opponent colors) + opponent cards − your cards

If both players reach 5 colors at the same time, the player who played the last card wins the round. A round win can never award negative points.

Playing the game

The round starts with 3 shared columns. Cards in the middle create shared columns, and each player can build on both sides, but only the bottom one is their own. The two sides can develop into different colors.

On your turn, play one card or draw 2 cards.

If the draw pile runs out and noone reached 5 colors, the round ends immediately in a draw (0 points for both).

Jokers do not count toward the 5 different colors, nor do the Black blocking cards.

Keep in mind that red, yellow and blue come up more often, while orange, green and purple are less common.

Card effects

Single-color card
Starts a new shared column in that color for both players.
Two-color transition
Can be played on a matching color. The first color is what it needs; the second color is what that side becomes. It can also be played on a Joker to determine the color of that column. If a card of this type starts a new column, the second color is active.
★
Rainbow Joker
Can start a new shared wild column. While unresolved, it does not count toward the 5 different colors. It can also reopen one blocked side as wild. If a column starts with Black, the Joker is placed on one side only while the other side remains blocked.
White card
Starts a new shared column with the top card of the draw pile. If another White is revealed, draw again. You may then optionally play one legal card from your hand on either side of that new column only.
×
Black blocking card
Blocks one side of a column, doesn't matter which card is at the end of it. You may not block a player who currently has less than 3 open columns. A blocked side can be reopened with a Joker or a Black transition card.
